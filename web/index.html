How to play

The goal

Fly as long as you can: the clock at the top counts your time in this level, in minutes and seconds, from 0:00 in every level. Dodge what is in your way: hot-air balloons, launch pads and huts on the coast; gulls and lifeguard towers at the beach. You have 3 lives; each crash costs one.

At the end of a level the autopilot lands on a runway: level complete! That unlocks the next level. Each level keeps your 5 best times.

Steering

Phone: turn it like a steering wheel to climb or dive. Tip it forward for more speed, back to slow down. Or drag your finger to where the plane should fly.

Keys: ↑↓ or WS climb and dive, →← or DA faster and slower. Or hold the mouse button: the plane flies to the pointer.

Calibration matters: how you hold the phone when you tap counts as "level". If the plane drifts, pause and tap Calibrate.

Watch your speed

OVERSPEED: too fast. Slow down within 3 seconds, or the plane breaks apart and every life is gone.

PULL UP: too slow. The plane sags; give gas, or it crashes into the ground.

Pause

The ☰ button (or Esc / Pswitching apps) pauses the game: Continue, Restart, Calibrate, Options or back to the Menu.
